### Dish costing and gross profit analysis

Slug: `dish-costing-and-gross-profit-analysis` → /services/kitchen-and-cost/dish-costing-and-gross-profit-analysis/

**Summary (hero supporting line):** We cost every dish from your own invoices and show you which ones make money and which ones lose it.

**Statement (body section heading):** Most menus carry a dish that loses money every time it sells.

**What it looks like:** The dining room is busy, the card machine keeps going, and there's still nothing left at the end of the month. When nobody has costed the menu against today's invoices, nobody knows which dishes are quietly paying for the others.

**What we do:** We cost your dishes from your own invoices — every ingredient, at the price you actually pay for it. Then we set each dish’s cost against its menu price, so you can see the gross profit on every line of the menu.

**What you leave with:** A costed menu, a list of the dishes that need a new price, a new recipe or a goodbye, and the order we’d tackle them in.

**Statement (body section heading):** Supplier prices creep. Nobody rings you to say so.

**What it looks like:** Prices move a little at a time — a few pence on the invoice here, a pack size change there. Order by order it's invisible. Over a year it's real money, and it never comes back on its own.
